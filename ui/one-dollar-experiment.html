How this experiment works

  1. Choose to participate with one voluntary $1 contribution.
  2. Stripe confirms the payment before the public counter changes.
  3. Refunds and disputes are deducted from proceeds.
  4. Actual payment processing fees are reconciled before net proceeds are calculated.
  5. Net proceeds are split 50/50 and reported separately.

This is not a charity, charitable donation, investment, loan, or securities offering. Contributions are not represented as tax-deductible. No financial return, ownership, product, or service is promised in exchange.
