# CYVX Field Manual Brand Kit

## Brand

**Name:** CYVX FIELD MANUAL  
**Tagline:** Build. Secure. Sell. Own.  
**Positioning:** Cinematic visual playbooks that turn cybersecurity, engineering, AI, sales, business systems, ownership, capital, and Web3 into executable action.

The brand is not a general motivation page. Each publication must contain a concrete outcome, bounded steps, a control, a verification procedure, and a measurable call to action.

## Identity system

### Wordmark

Use `CYVX FIELD MANUAL` in uppercase, heavy display type. The preferred lockup places `BUILD • SECURE • SELL • OWN` underneath in small monospaced text.

### Monogram

`CFM` is the compact mark for avatars, watermarks, and module fingerprints. Do not use it without the full brand name on first exposure.

### Clear space

Preserve at least one monogram width around the wordmark. Do not place the mark over detailed imagery without a dark contrast panel.

## Typography

- Display: Arial Black, Inter Black, or a comparable heavy grotesk.
- Body: Inter or system sans-serif.
- Technical labels: JetBrains Mono or system monospace.

Headlines are short, outcome-first, and usually 3–8 words. Supporting text uses normal sentence case.

## Visual language

- Matte black industrial environments
- One central symbolic object
- Precise grid, technical measurements, module identifiers, and evidence fingerprints
- Restrained glow rather than saturated gamer effects
- Large negative space for readable mobile headlines
- Repeating numbered action frames
- Evidence panels for time, cost, difficulty, risk, and verification

Avoid copied layouts, anonymous-hooded-hacker clichés, fake terminal output, tiny decorative code, unverified dashboards, exaggerated money imagery, and more than one dominant accent.

## Voice

The voice is direct, precise, credible, ambitious, and evidence-first.

Every module must:

1. Lead with a concrete outcome.
2. Use plain language and numbered actions.
3. Separate verified facts from estimates.
4. Avoid guaranteed income, returns, security, or legal outcomes.
5. End with a measurable verification step.
6. Include a limitation or disclaimer when the topic is consequential.

## Content anatomy

1. **Cover:** Stop-scroll outcome and visual symbol.
2. **Outcome contract:** Time, cost, difficulty, and finished state.
3. **Prepare:** Inputs, prerequisites, and access.
4. **Execute:** Core action.
5. **Control:** Security, legal, financial, or operational safeguard.
6. **Verify:** Evidence that proves the system works.
7. **CTA:** Save, comment keyword, download, or join a relevant implementation path.

## File and naming rules

- Module ID: `CFM-###`
- Post slug: lowercase kebab case
- Slide file: `slide-01.svg`
- Export file: `###-slug-slide-01.png`
- Campaign: short kebab case
- Every generated artifact receives a SHA-256 entry in the build manifest.

## Channel rules

### Carousel

- 1080 × 1350
- Seven slides preferred
- Maximum one idea per slide
- Body copy must remain readable on a 6-inch phone

### Reels and Shorts

- 45–75 seconds
- Show the outcome in the first two seconds
- Use the approved reel script as the factual boundary
- Add captions and visible step numbers
- End on verification and one CTA

### Caption

- Restate the problem and finished outcome
- Include the numbered sequence
- State time, cost, and difficulty
- Include the limitation
- Use one primary CTA

## Monetization alignment

Free modules solve the first layer of the problem. Paid products should add depth through templates, automation, implementation, maintenance, measurement, or industry-specific adaptation—not by removing necessary context from the free content.

## Governance

No publication may invent customers, testimonials, case studies, revenue, savings, security results, audits, endorsements, credentials, or sources. High-impact instructions require a control and a verification step. External messaging, financial actions, contracts, and destructive operations remain approval-gated.
