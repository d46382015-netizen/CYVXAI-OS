# CYVXAI OS Support Operations

## Intake

Support requests enter through a tracked GitHub issue or the designated support channel. Every request receives an owner, severity, environment, reproduction evidence, affected user or tenant, and expected next update.

## Response targets

- SEV-1: acknowledge within 15 minutes and maintain active incident ownership.
- SEV-2: acknowledge within one hour.
- SEV-3: acknowledge within one business day.

## Escalation

Escalate immediately for suspected credential exposure, unauthorized access, lost or corrupted data, payment-impacting defects, failed backups, or a production outage. Product questions and feature requests remain outside the incident channel unless they expose an operational risk.

## Closure

A request closes only after the outcome is verified with the reporter or with reproducible system evidence. Link the fixing commit, release, tests, and production verification. Convert repeated support issues into automation, documentation, validation, or product changes.
