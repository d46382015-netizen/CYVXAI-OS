Data use

Plain-language privacy notice.

When you request the Operator Starter Manual, the system stores the email address, optional name, selected interest, consent timestamp, campaign source, and a one-way hash of the submitting IP address. Runtime events record page, post, CTA, export, and download activity without intentionally storing message content or financial data.

Purpose

The data is used to deliver the requested material, operate the publication system, understand which modules are useful, and send related Field Manual updates to people who consented.

Storage

The default self-hosted runtime stores leads and event logs under the operator-controlled CYVX data directory. Production operators must configure appropriate access control, encryption, backups, retention, unsubscribe handling, and legally required disclosures before public launch.

Your choices

Do not submit the form when you do not consent. A production deployment must provide a working contact and unsubscribe process before sending campaigns.
